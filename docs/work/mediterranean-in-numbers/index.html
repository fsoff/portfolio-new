A sea in numbers

Practice
Data visualisation · Infographics

Giving marine statistics a tangible form. A series of infographics makes the scale of aquaculture, small-scale fisheries, marine energy and nature protection visible.

The figures and their sources remain part of each original graphic. These are a record of the data used in the project, rather than a live statistical dashboard.
