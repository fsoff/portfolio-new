Plastissea

Practice
Illustration · Children’s book · Storytelling

An exploration at sea, for children and the adults reading with them. Drawing, language and scientific knowledge come together in an illustrated story about the marine world and plastic.

Text, illustrations and graphic design by Folco Soffietti. Created with support from the European Union through POSEIDONE, Interreg VI-A Italy–Slovenia, with CORILA, Università Iuav di Venezia and CNR-Ismar. ISBN 9788889405475.
