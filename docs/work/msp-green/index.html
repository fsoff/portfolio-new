Communicating the Maritime Green Deal

Practice
Editorial design · Policy communication

Making the connections between marine planning and the European Green Deal easier to follow. Editorial design and infographics turn policy relationships into a visual companion for practitioners, decision-makers and sustainability communicators.

Report by Annija Danenberga and Folco Soffietti, developed within MSP-GREEN. Infographics by Folco Soffietti; photographs by Annija Danenberga. The selection also includes visual summaries of Green Deal components in maritime spatial plans.
