A way of working

Different tools.
The same attention to what matters.

I think of myself as a narrative artisan. Whether creating a diagram, a digital interface, a graphic novel or a sculpture, the medium follows the questions and the story they trigger.

My research experience at Università Iuav di Venezia shapes an approach grounded in careful observation, clear communication and a willingness to experiment.
